# About Edinburgh Folk Club

Edinburgh Folk Club has been home to folk nights in the capital since **1973**.

For more than fifty years the club has brought traditional and contemporary folk music to Edinburgh audiences — welcoming visiting artists from across Scotland and further afield, and giving a stage to the city’s own musicians.

We meet regularly for live performances in an intimate, welcoming setting. Whether you are a lifelong folk devotee or curious for the first time, you will find a warm welcome at EFC.
