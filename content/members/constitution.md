# Club Constitution

This page will hold the Edinburgh Folk Club constitution for members.

*Placeholder document for the Phase 1 prototype.*

## 1. Name

The club shall be known as Edinburgh Folk Club.

## 2. Objects

The objects of the club are to promote and encourage interest in folk music through live performance and related activities.

## 3. Membership

Membership is open to all who support the objects of the club, subject to payment of the annual subscription as set by the committee.
