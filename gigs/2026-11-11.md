Pete Morton began his musical journey after he came across a Buffy St Marie record. It was from that moment on he decided to become a folk singer. Discovering the songs of the early sixties protest movement, it inspired him to write, and take his songs to folk clubs and beyond.

For thirty-plus years, Pete has been performing to audience all over the world. Throughout that time he has been regarded as one of the best on the contemporary roots music scene. His album ‘A Golden Thread’ received rave reviews with eight self penned songs, a Pete Seeger classic ‘Oh Had I A Golden Thread’, and versions of the traditional gems ‘Barbry Allen’ and ‘The Farmers Boy’. That, and the follow-up Fair Freedom (2024), are the last Pete recorded for he no longer records albums, now concentrating on live performance. Although still writing, his new work is included in a Woody Guthrie-like talking blues style 'performance only' show that makes every gig unique to the audience.

Throughout the early '90s, Pete toured solo throughout the US, Canada, and Europe, as well as touring with Roger Wilson (fiddle, vocals, guitar) and Simon Edwards (button accordion, vocals). They also toured Pakistan, Malaysia, and Singapore for the British Council.

Often referred to as an old time troubadour, Pete has a compelling stage presence and approachable style that delivers an unruly mix of humanism, politics, love, social commentary and humour, all wrapping their way around the folk tradition.

This is not Pete's first visit to Edinburgh FC - far from it - and no matter what his ‘style’ of performance we have always enjoyed his humour, lyrics, music and performances so we're looking forward this next visit.
