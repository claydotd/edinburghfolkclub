# Edinburgh Folk Club Constitution

## 1. Name
The name of the club shall be EDINBURGH FOLK CLUB hereinafter referred to as "the Club" or “EFC”. 

## 2. Aims
The aims of the Club are: 
To encourage the preservation, study and appreciation of folk song and music, particularly in the Scottish traditional idiom. To achieve this EFC will organise and arrange concerts and other events in the folk idiom, co-operate with other bodies with similar aims and carry out other actions which may support this aim.

## 3. Membership
Membership of EFC will be open to those who have paid the appropriate annual subscription. Only members of at least eight weeks standing will have voting rights. The amount of the subscription will be fixed from time to time by the committee. The committee will have the right to offer Life Membership which will be free of charge.
All members must comply with this constitution.
The committee may revoke membership at its discretion.

## 4. Management Committee
The Club will be managed by a Committee elected at the Annual General Meeting (AGM).
The Committee shall consist of at least a Chair, Secretary, and Treasurer. Up to five additional members may be co-opted as needs arise. All Committee members may vote at meetings.
Committee members shall hold office until the next AGM and may then stand for re-election.
The Committee will meet at least not less than nine times per year. 

## 5. Meetings
Annual General Meeting (AGM): The AGM will be held once a year in September to receive reports, elect the committee, and vote on any changes to the constitution. Written notice of the AGM and its agenda will be circulated to members not less than four weeks before the AGM. Changes to this constitution of the Club may be made at an AGM or an Extraordinary General Meeting (see below).
Extraordinary General Meeting: An extraordinary general meeting can be called by the committee or on written request of six members to discuss urgent business. 
A quorum at both regular Committee Meetings and General Meetings must consist of three committee members of whom two will be office bearers.
Voting: All members have the right to vote at the AGM or an EGM. Decisions are made by a simple majority of those present, subject to the provisions of Rule 7.

## 6. Finances
A bank account shall be maintained in the name of the Club.
The Treasurer will keep accounts and report at the AGM.
Money may be spent only for the aims of the Club.
Committee members may not receive payments from the Club, except to refund reasonable expenses. 

## 7. Amendments to the Constitution 
This constitution can be amended only at an AGM or Special General Meeting, requiring a two-thirds majority of members present at the meeting. 

## 8. Affiliation
EFC may form affiliations as appropriate to further the aims of the club.

## 9. Dissolution
If the committee decides to close the Club, it must call an EGM.
Any assets remaining after paying debts must be distributed to another group with similar objectives as determined by the membership at the EGM.
