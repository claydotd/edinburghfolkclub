 # Minutes of the Annual General Meeting, Ukrainian Community Centre, 16/09/2026

1.	Present:  Jim Welsh, Jim Farley, John Barrow, Pamela King, Keith Cocks, Alan Dudley.

2.	Apologies for absence: none registered.

3.	Chair’s Report: to follow.

4.	Treasurer’s Report: The Treasurer presented and spoke to  a spreadsheet (attached)  of financial transactions in the period March-July 2026. The Folk Club is in a stable financial position.

5.	Secretary's Report: Vacant Position.

6.	Programme for season 2026-27: The meeting received the programme of bookings for 2026-27, as published on the website and in print media.

7.	Membership: It was agreed that there should henceforth be only individual membership at £25 per season. Members should receive entry to meetings of the Club at a flat rate of £10 per person.

8.	Election of Office bearers: Chair (joint) Jim Welsh and Pamela King; Treasurer Graham Ritchie; Secretary vacant; Co-option Jim Farley, Tim Martin.
	All elected nem. con.
It was further agreed that John Barrow should be appointed non-executive Honorary President in recognition of his unique contributions to the Folk Club during its entire history.
